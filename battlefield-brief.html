Division 1

Is the situation really that bad?

The Hook

It is a tough time to be a teenager in India.

Let us look at the facts in 2026. Rent is going up. Groceries cost more every month. The money in your pocket buys less than it did a year ago. If you are young right now, you are stuck in a brutal education race where over 600 million people are fighting for a small number of good jobs. And to make it worse, many of those entry-level jobs, the basic coding work, copywriting, and data entry, are being replaced by machines before you even finish your degree.

This is not a phase that will pass. The stress is real at every age. 24-year-olds are panicking because the rules keep changing. 34-year-olds are worn out from running towards a finish line that keeps moving. Air quality in big cities is getting worse. Companies are laying people off every week. The pressure you feel when you wake up in the morning is not in your head. Things are genuinely harder. The ground is unstable, competition is fierce, and the old systems that used to protect people are gone.

Face the situation for what it is. But understand this: knowing it is hard does not mean you get to stop trying.

Division 2

Why did the old plan stop working?

The Harsh Truth

The old plan is dead. The idea that you study hard, get a degree, find a stable job, and everything works out is no longer true.

The economy has changed in a big way. Inflation, global shocks, and AI tools have all hit at the same time. Companies today do not need large teams of average workers. They want smart systems and a small number of skilled people to run them. If your plan is to wait for things to calm down, or hope the job market becomes fair again, you are falling behind right now. For millennials, this feels like a broken promise. For Gen Z, this is just the world they were born into.

And let us be honest about how most people are dealing with this. Spending hours scrolling social media, watching videos about how unfair the system is, and posting angry comments is not activism. It is a way to avoid doing anything. It lets you feel like you care without actually changing anything. Complaining about the system, or sitting stuck in a job that is going nowhere, is just giving up while pretending it is someone else's fault.

The system does not care about your complaints. It is not going to fix itself for you. You are either changing and growing, or you are getting left behind.

Division 3

What is your phone doing to you?

The Social Media Trap

Let us talk about the biggest thing working against you every single day: the phone in your hand. Social media is not a way to relax. It is not a tool for real connection. It is a product built to keep you hooked.

Every time you open an app, you are giving your attention to a system designed by some of the world's best engineers for one purpose: to stop you from thinking clearly and make money from your time. When you spend hours watching short videos, your brain gets rewired. You start finding it hard to sit with a textbook, work through a difficult problem, or focus on anything for more than a minute without picking up your phone. You are slowly damaging your own ability to think deeply.

On top of that, you are hurting yourself by constantly comparing your life to what you see online. You sit in traffic in a polluted city, staring at polished, fake highlights of other people who look like they are winning at life. This builds up a loop of anxiety and inaction. You feel behind before you have even started, so you go back to scrolling to feel better for a few minutes.

Here is the real irony: while you complain that big companies are stealing your future through automation, you are giving those same companies your attention completely for free, every single day.

Division 4

So what do you actually do?

The Next Level

Stop waiting to feel passionate about something. Passion is a bonus when things are going well. Right now, what you need is to be useful, skilled, and consistent. That means making real changes to how you spend your time every day.

  • Build Skills That Cannot Be Automated If an AI can do your job with a simple prompt, you can be replaced. Focus instead on learning fast, solving hard problems, and working well with people in messy, real-world situations where software falls short. These are the things that still require a human.
  • Protect Your Focus Your ability to concentrate is your most valuable tool. If you cannot focus for several hours without checking your phone, you are already at a disadvantage. Use your phone to create and produce, not to scroll and consume. Turn off notifications. Delete what is distracting you. Guard your attention like it matters, because it does.
  • Stop Waiting for the Right Moment The comfortable era is over. Wanting everything to be easy and rewarding right away is what holds most people back. Build real, visible work that anyone can see online instead of collecting certificates that prove very little. Do not wait until conditions are perfect. They never will be.

Change how you think, take action, and become someone that cannot be ignored.
