Who's Facing the Most Criminal Cases in the Lok Sabha?

Every sitting Lok Sabha MP with a digitized criminal-case entry on their election affidavit, mapped by state. Every figure below links back to its source.

Shivam Kumar | Loading dataset…
Read this before the map: these are cases filed against an MP in their sworn ECI affidavit, not convictions. Under Indian law, an accused is presumed innocent until proven guilty. This covers 484 of 543 winning candidates: the rest did not have a digitized criminal-case entry in the source list at the time of collection, so they are left out rather than guessed at. Zero declared cases is shown as a plain fact, not a "good" score. The map colors each state by its true average across every MP counted there, since this is now a full count of that state's MPs, not a small sample, so an average is statistically sound. Source for every number: MyNeta / ADR, based on public ECI election affidavits. The first 18 rows (the highest case counts) were individually cross-checked against their affidavit pages; the rest are sourced directly from MyNeta's published list, each linked to its own affidavit page for independent verification. Data collected: 27 July 2026. MyNeta records can change after court dispositions, so treat this as a point-in-time snapshot, not a live feed.
